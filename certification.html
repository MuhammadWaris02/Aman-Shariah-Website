Why Certify

Confidence For Every Stakeholder

Aman Shariah Advisory provides Shariah assessment and certification for eligible products, services, contracts, financial structures, and business models.

Our certification process is designed to provide stakeholders with confidence that the reviewed subject has been assessed against relevant Shariah principles and approved conditions.

What Can Be Certified?

  • Financial & investment products, takaful products
  • Commercial contracts & business models
  • Digital platforms & Islamic financing structures
  • Funds, portfolios & real estate arrangements
  • Training programmes, policies & charitable/waqf structures

Documentation

Documents Generally Required

Depending on the assignment, applicants may be required to provide the following.

Company Profile Incorporation Documents Ownership Information Business Model & Transaction Flow Product Concept Paper Agreements & Contracts Policies & Procedures Financial Projections Marketing Materials Regulatory Approvals Sample Transactions Previous Shariah Opinions

Certificate Validity

The validity of a certificate may depend on:

  • The scope of review & continued compliance with approved documentation
  • Absence of material changes & periodic reporting
  • Annual review, renewal, and certification conditions

Any significant change in the product, contract, business process, ownership, or transaction structure may require further review.

Certification Disclaimer: A Shariah certificate is issued within the defined scope of review and based on the documents and information submitted by the applicant. Certification does not replace regulatory, legal, tax, accounting, technical, or commercial approvals that may otherwise be required.
