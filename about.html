Who We Are

A Reliable, Independent Advisory Partner

Aman Shariah Advisory is a professional advisory organisation established to provide reliable, independent, and practical Shariah guidance to businesses, financial institutions, entrepreneurs, investors, and nonprofit organisations.

The firm seeks to bridge the gap between traditional Islamic jurisprudence and the requirements of modern commerce. Our objective is to help clients adopt Shariah-compliant structures without compromising commercial practicality, operational efficiency, regulatory expectations, or long-term sustainability.

We believe Shariah compliance is not limited to avoiding prohibited elements — it also promotes fairness, transparency, responsible conduct, ethical investment, accountability, and protection of stakeholder interests.

Our Vision

A Trusted Institution For Ethical Business

To become a trusted Shariah advisory institution recognised for professional excellence, reliable certification, practical solutions, and meaningful contribution to the development of ethical and Shariah-compliant businesses.

Our Mission

Practical Compliance, Real Support

To assist organisations in developing and maintaining Shariah-compliant products, services, transactions, governance systems, and business practices through qualified advisory, independent review, certification, training, and continuous compliance support.
